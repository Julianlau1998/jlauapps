AdNumbers: AdMob Dashboard

AdMob & AdSense Earnings

Your ad revenue. At a glance.

AdNumbers shows your earnings from Google AdMob and Google AdSense in a clean, fast dashboard – designed like an Apple app, for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

iPhoneiPadMacApple WatchFree

Features

AN OVERVIEW THAT THINKS AHEAD

  • Today, yesterday, 7 days, month and year – each compared with the previous period
  • Forecast for the current month and a monthly goal with progress
  • AdMob and AdSense separately or combined
  • Show, hide and reorder sections
  • Record day or goal reached? Celebrate it – and share a success card as an image

EVERY NUMBER IN DETAIL

  • Interactive bar or line charts with previous period and previous year
  • Earnings, impressions, eCPM, clicks, CTR, requests, match rate and show rate
  • Breakdowns by apps, websites, ad units, countries, formats, platforms and ad networks
  • Every app, country and period has its own detail page
  • Custom periods, search, sorting and CSV export
  • AdSense balance and payments

STATISTICS

  • Calendar heatmap, monthly history with forecast and month comparison
  • eCPM trend, strongest weekdays and distribution by source and platform
  • Choose the period of every chart

MAKE IT YOURS

  • Light and dark mode, background color of your choice
  • "Hide Amounts" when you show the app to others
  • Supports larger text and VoiceOver
  • On iPad and Mac with sidebar, multiple columns and keyboard shortcuts

ON-DEVICE AI ANALYSIS

With Apple Intelligence, AdNumbers analyzes your numbers: what changed, why – and what you can do about it. Concrete suggestions for apps and websites, for the overview, single apps, countries or periods. The analysis runs entirely on your device.

ALWAYS WITH YOU

  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets – switch the period right in the widget
  • A widget for your monthly goal
  • Apple Watch app with complications
  • Siri and Shortcuts: "How much did I earn with AdNumbers?"
  • Notifications with a daily summary, record days and sharp drops

PRIVATE

AdNumbers only requests read-only access to your reports. Your data goes straight from Google to your device – no server of our own. Your earnings and reports are never shared, not even for advertising.

Try it without an account: tap "Try the Demo" on the start screen.

AdNumbers is not an official Google product. AdMob and AdSense are trademarks of Google LLC.

PRO

ADNUMBERS PRO

The overview, countries, formats, platforms, Siri and notifications are free, plus the top entry of your apps and ad units. AdNumbers Pro adds every app and ad unit, AI analysis, statistics, widgets and the Apple Watch app, custom periods, CSV export and background colors – and removes all ads. Monthly or yearly, cancel anytime.
