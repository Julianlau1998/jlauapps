3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Apps show ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. In particular, the following data may be processed: IP address, device and system information (such as device model, operating system version, language), approximate location (derived from the IP address), interactions with ads and – if you have consented – your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android).

Legal basis and consent: Personalized ads are only shown with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Where required, you are asked through Google's User Messaging Platform or – on iOS – Apple's tracking prompt. Without consent, only non-personalized ads are shown; for these, Google still processes technically necessary data (such as the IP address) to deliver ads, prevent fraud and create statistics (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest in funding the free Apps).

Withdrawal: On iOS, you can withdraw the tracking permission at any time in the Settings under Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in the device settings under Google › Ads.

Data may be transferred to countries outside the EU, in particular the United States. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
