Connect Insights: Analytics

Downloads, Subscriptions & ASO

Your App Store numbers. At a glance.

Connect Insights shows downloads, proceeds, subscriptions, keyword rankings and App Store analytics for all your apps in a clean, fast dashboard – designed with Liquid Glass for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

iPhoneiPadMacApple WatchFree

Features

SUBSCRIPTIONS, LIVE

  • New subscriptions, trials, renewals and cancellations within minutes – not a day later
  • Active subscriptions and free trials over time
  • How many free trials convert to paid, per product
  • Cancellations with date and reason
  • Payouts per month and currency
  • Refunds, promo codes and pre-orders

APP STORE ANALYTICS

  • Sessions, active devices, deletions and crashes – also by version
  • Where your downloads come from: search, browse, websites, other apps
  • For all apps together, per app and per country

EVERY APP IN DETAIL

  • Trends, countries, products and every status: in review, rejected, pending release and more
  • Performance per version: launch time, hangs, memory and battery
  • In-app events, custom product pages, product page tests, prices and offers
  • Reviews from every country – reply right in the app
  • TestFlight builds, testers and feedback

ASO: SEE WHERE YOU RANK

  • Keyword rankings for your apps in many countries – found automatically
  • A visibility score per app with its history and your top chart position
  • Concrete suggestions for the title, subtitle and keyword field of every localization
  • Ratings per country with their history

OVERVIEW

  • Downloads, proceeds, sales, active subscriptions, free trials and purchases – compared with the previous period or year
  • Interactive charts for every metric
  • Top apps and top countries by downloads or proceeds, proceeds also per product
  • Monthly goals with a forecast and alerts for unusual days
  • Filter by apps, countries and period, amounts in your currency

ALWAYS WITH YOU

  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • Apple Watch app with complications
  • Notifications for new subscriptions, reviews and star ratings, unusual days and a morning summary
  • Siri and Shortcuts: "Connect Insights downloads"
  • CSV export, a menu bar summary and keyboard shortcuts on the Mac

PRIVATE

Connect Insights uses your own API key for the App Store Connect API. It stays in your device's keychain, and your data goes straight from Apple to your device – no server of our own. The app doesn't change anything in App Store Connect except what you trigger yourself: requesting the analytics reports once and replying to reviews.

Try it without an account: tap "Explore the Demo First" at launch.

Connect Insights is not an official Apple product. App Store, App Store Connect and TestFlight are trademarks of Apple Inc.

PRO

CONNECT INSIGHTS PRO

The overview, apps, countries, subscriptions and app status are free, plus ASO for your most important app and the 7- and 30-day periods. Pro adds live subscriptions, App Store analytics, widgets and Apple Watch, notifications, goals, payouts, review replies, ASO for every app, listing suggestions, longer periods and CSV export – without ads. Monthly or yearly, cancel anytime.

Frequently asked questions

How does Connect Insights get my App Store Connect data?

Connect Insights uses your own API key for the App Store Connect API. The key stays in your device's keychain, and your data goes straight from Apple to your device without a server of its own.

Can Connect Insights change anything in App Store Connect?

Only what you trigger yourself: requesting the analytics reports once and replying to reviews. Otherwise the app doesn't change anything in App Store Connect.

Can I try Connect Insights without an API key?

Yes. Tap “Explore the Demo First” at launch to try it without an account.

Is Connect Insights free, and what does Pro add?

The overview, apps, countries, subscriptions and app status are free, plus ASO for your most important app and the 7- and 30-day periods. Pro adds live subscriptions, App Store analytics, widgets and Apple Watch, notifications, goals, payouts, review replies, ASO for every app, listing suggestions, longer periods and CSV export, without ads. It's monthly or yearly, each with a free one-week trial.

Does Connect Insights show new subscriptions in real time?

With Pro, new subscriptions, trials, renewals and cancellations show up within minutes instead of a day later. Connect Insights runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, and Pro also sends notifications for new subscriptions.
