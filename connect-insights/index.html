Connect Insights: Analytics

ASO & Sales for App Developers

ASO and App Store analytics for app developers – in one native app.

Connect Insights shows where your apps rank, what to improve next and what it brings: keyword rankings, search demand, competitors and your listing – next to downloads, proceeds and live subscriptions. Designed with Liquid Glass for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

iPhoneiPadMacApple WatchFree

Features

KEYWORD RESEARCH

  • Any search term: demand, difficulty, competing apps and who ranks on top
  • Related searches, ideas from your competitors and opportunities your app already fits
  • Track a term for any of your apps in one tap

COMPETITORS

  • Watch competitors' versions, prices, ratings and name changes
  • See the keywords where they beat you, and where your own apps compete with each other

LISTING OPTIMIZATION

  • Concrete suggestions for title, subtitle and keyword field of every localization
  • Keyword field builder that saves a draft to the version you're preparing
  • Title ideas, the texts in your screenshots and localizations that add room for keywords

YOUR NUMBERS

  • Downloads, proceeds, sales, subscriptions and trials – compared with the previous period or year
  • New subscriptions, trials and cancellations live within minutes
  • MRR, churn, trial conversion, payouts and refunds
  • Impressions, product page views, conversion and where downloads come from
  • App status, performance per version, TestFlight builds and feedback
  • Reviews from every country with on-device summaries and reply suggestions
  • Release markers and notes in every chart, monthly goals with a forecast

ASO THAT TELLS YOU WHAT TO DO

  • Keyword rankings in every country you choose – keywords are found automatically
  • Next best moves: the keywords with the most to gain, a realistic target rank and roughly how many downloads that brings
  • Search downloads next to your visibility, so you see whether better rankings pay off
  • Demand trends: terms people search more or less often than a few weeks ago
  • Country opportunities: where you already get downloads without a localized listing
  • Listing changes with rankings and search downloads before and after
  • Visibility score, category chart rank and the apps right around you

ALWAYS WITH YOU

  • Widgets, Apple Watch app and complications
  • Notifications for subscriptions, reviews, rankings and competitor updates
  • Siri and Shortcuts, CSV export, a menu bar summary on the Mac

PRIVATE

Connect Insights uses your own key for the App Store Connect API. It stays in your device's keychain, and your data goes straight from Apple to your device – no server of our own. For ASO alone you don't need a key: search for your developer name. The app only changes something in App Store Connect when you trigger it: requesting the analytics reports once, replying to reviews and saving a keyword draft.

Try it without an account: tap "Explore the Demo First" at launch.

Connect Insights is not an official Apple product. App Store, App Store Connect and TestFlight are trademarks of Apple Inc.

PRO

FREE AND PRO

Free: the overview, apps, countries, subscriptions and app status, ASO for your most downloaded app, 5 keyword lookups a day and one competitor per app. Pro adds ASO for every app, unlimited keyword research and competitors, listing optimization, live subscriptions, App Store analytics, MRR and churn, widgets and Apple Watch, notifications, goals, payouts, review replies, longer periods and CSV export – without ads.

Frequently asked questions

How does Connect Insights get my App Store Connect data?

Connect Insights uses your own API key for the App Store Connect API. The key stays in your device's keychain, and your data goes straight from Apple to your device without a server of its own.

Can Connect Insights change anything in App Store Connect?

Only what you trigger yourself: requesting the analytics reports once and replying to reviews. Otherwise the app doesn't change anything in App Store Connect.

Can I try Connect Insights without an API key?

Yes. Tap “Explore the Demo First” at launch to try it without an account.

Is Connect Insights free, and what does Pro add?

The overview, apps, countries, subscriptions and app status are free, plus ASO for your most important app and the 7- and 30-day periods. Pro adds live subscriptions, App Store analytics, widgets and Apple Watch, notifications, goals, payouts, review replies, ASO for every app, listing suggestions, longer periods and CSV export, without ads. It's monthly or yearly, each with a free one-week trial.

Does Connect Insights show new subscriptions in real time?

With Pro, new subscriptions, trials, renewals and cancellations show up within minutes instead of a day later. Connect Insights runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, and Pro also sends notifications for new subscriptions.
