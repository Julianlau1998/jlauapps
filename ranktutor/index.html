RankTutor: SEO Website Check

Your step-by-step Google plan

Your website should bring you customers. RankTutor checks it in about a minute, gives it a score from 0 to 100 and tells you in plain words what to fix next – so more people find you on Google and in AI answers like ChatGPT. Made for small business owners, freelancers and anyone who runs a website without an SEO team.

Features

WEBSITE CHECK

  • 45 checks in six categories: technical, on-page, performance & mobile, social & structured data, security and local SEO
  • Speed and Core Web Vitals from Google PageSpeed Insights
  • Fix next: the fixes with the biggest effect first, each with a clear explanation and step-by-step instructions
  • A summary in plain words, written by Apple Intelligence on your device (on supported devices)
  • Local SEO for shops, restaurants and practices: address, phone, opening hours and map link
  • Ready-made code for structured data, meta tags and security headers

YOUR GOOGLE NUMBERS

  • Connect Google Search Console with read-only access: clicks, impressions, click rate and position compared with the period before
  • Your plan for this week: the tasks that bring the most clicks, with an estimate
  • Recommendations: searches almost on page one, pages with a weak click rate, pages losing clicks and rising searches
  • Tracked keywords: your Google position for the searches that matter, day by day
  • Notes on Google updates, experiments to measure your changes, sitemap status and index status

AI SEARCH

  • AI answers: see every week whether ChatGPT recommends you, who it names instead and which websites it relies on
  • AI crawlers: can ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity read your website? Plus an llms.txt generator
  • Ask RankTutor: an assistant that knows your check and your Google numbers

MORE TOOLS

Google result preview, keyword check, content gaps, content plan, internal links, image audit, backlink watch, Bing and IndexNow, competitor comparison, PDF report, Home Screen widget, Shortcuts and Siri, and checking a website right from Safari.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

  • Website checks run on your device – no account needed
  • Search Console access is read-only; your Google data stays on your device
  • Questions go to ChatGPT only if you allow it

In English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Japanese and Korean, for iPhone and iPad.

RankTutor is not affiliated with Google or OpenAI.
