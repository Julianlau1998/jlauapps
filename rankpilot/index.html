About RankpilotBot

Rankpilot loads websites from the user's own device, identified as RankpilotBot/1.0 (+https://jlauapps.com/rankpilot/). It reads robots.txt and follows it, loads at most 100 pages per check and waits between requests. To block it, add User-agent: RankpilotBot and Disallow: / to your robots.txt.
