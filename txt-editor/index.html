Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account for the txt Editor notepad app?

No. txt Editor opens in an instant and gives you a blank page, with no account and no setup. Everything saves automatically as you write.

Where does txt Editor save my notes?

Your notes are plain .txt files in the Files app under “On My iPhone › txt Editor”, so nothing is locked in. You can share them to any app.

Which file types can txt Editor open?

You can open text files from iCloud Drive or other apps and edit them right where they are – .txt, .md, .csv, .json, .xml, .log and more.

Does txt Editor sync notes between iPhone and iPad?

Yes, with txt Editor Pro: through iCloud sync, changes show up on iPhone and iPad within seconds.

Is txt Editor free, and what does Pro add?

Writing, folders, search, find and replace and the word and character count work without a subscription. Pro adds no ads, iCloud sync, Markdown with a preview, eight designs, widgets, version history, and an app lock and private folder with Face ID, as a monthly or yearly subscription.
