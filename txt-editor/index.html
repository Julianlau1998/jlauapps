Notes · for iPhone, iPad

txt Editor - Text Editor

Fast, distraction-free notes

Just start writing. txt Editor opens in an instant and gives you a blank page – no account, no setup, no clutter. For shopping lists, ideas, journaling, drafts or code snippets.

Free · In-app purchases

Features

AS FAST AS PAPER

Tap New Document, type, done. Everything saves automatically as you write, and the editor stays smooth even with long texts.

ORGANIZED YOUR WAY

  • Folders with their own color and icon
  • Pin what matters to the top
  • Sort by date, name, or your own order with drag and drop
  • Search finds every word across all your documents

REAL FILES, NOTHING LOCKED IN

Your notes are plain .txt files in the Files app under “On My iPhone › txt Editor”. You can also open text files from iCloud Drive or other apps and edit them right where they are – .txt, .md, .csv, .json, .xml, .log and more.

HANDY TOOLS

  • Find and replace
  • Words, characters and reading time at a glance
  • Choose your font and text size
  • Share to any app
