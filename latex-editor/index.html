Texlet: LaTeX Editor

Math, formulas & documents

Write formulas in LaTeX and see them typeset right away. Texlet opens in an instant – no account, no setup, no internet needed. For homework, lecture notes, slides, theses and every equation that should look good.

iPhoneiPadFree

Features

TYPE IT, SEE IT TYPESET

The live preview shows your formula as you write – right below it on iPhone, next to it on iPad, and on iPhone Duo half open, right on the other half. Or view it full screen, free of distractions. Fractions, integrals, sums, matrices, cases and chemistry with \ce are typeset cleanly, even offline.

BUILDING BLOCKS AT HAND

  • Above the keyboard: fraction, root, superscript and subscript, Greek letters, sums and integrals, relations, arrows, brackets, matrices and environments
  • Brackets close themselves
  • Return after \begin{…} adds the matching \end{…}, and lists continue with the next \item
  • Syntax highlighting for commands, math, environments and comments

REAL .TEX FILES

Your formulas are plain .tex files in the Files app under “On My iPhone › Texlet”. Open .tex files from iCloud Drive or other apps and edit them right where they are. Copy the LaTeX code with one tap.

ORGANIZED YOUR WAY

  • Every formula as a typeset thumbnail on the home screen
  • Folders with their own color and icon
  • Pin what matters, sort by date, name or your own order
  • Search, find and replace, printing and a LaTeX guide with examples

WHOLE DOCUMENTS, TOO

Put formulas in text with $…$, or write a whole document with \section, lists, tables and numbered equations – the preview typesets it beautifully, with title and sections. Your own \newcommand macros work too.

PRO

TEXLET PRO

For when you want more – as a monthly or yearly subscription:

  • No ads
  • Copy a formula as an image – sharp in Keynote, Pages, Notes and more
  • Export as PDF (vector) and PNG
  • iCloud sync between iPhone and iPad
  • Eight designs, from warm paper to deep black midnight
  • Widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen
  • Version history: bring back any earlier state
  • App lock and a private folder with Face ID

Texlet is built by an independent developer. If you enjoy it, a rating means a lot!

Frequently asked questions

Does the Texlet LaTeX editor work offline?

Yes. Texlet needs no account, no setup and no internet, and the live preview typesets your formulas even offline.

Can I write whole LaTeX documents in Texlet, or only formulas?

Both. You can put formulas in text with $…$ or write a whole document with \section, lists, tables and numbered equations, and your own \newcommand macros work too. Chemistry formulas with \ce are typeset as well.

Can Texlet export LaTeX formulas as PDF or PNG?

Yes, with Texlet Pro: you can export as PDF (vector) and PNG, and copy a formula as a sharp image for Keynote, Pages, Notes and more. Copying the LaTeX code itself takes one tap.

Is Texlet free, and what does Texlet Pro add?

Writing with the live preview, the building blocks above the keyboard and your .tex files work without a subscription. Pro adds no ads, image copy, PDF and PNG export, iCloud sync between iPhone and iPad, eight designs, widgets, version history, and an app lock and private folder with Face ID, as a monthly or yearly subscription.

Does Texlet work on iPad?

Yes. On iPad the live preview sits right next to your formula, and on iPhone Duo, half open, the result shows on the other half. With Pro, your formulas sync between iPhone and iPad through iCloud.
