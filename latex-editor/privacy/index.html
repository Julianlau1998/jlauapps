2. Deine Dokumente

Deine Dokumente werden ausschließlich auf deinem Gerät gespeichert – im Ordner „Auf meinem iPhone/iPad › LaTeX Editor“ der Dateien-App. Mit LaTeX Editor Pro kannst du sie zusätzlich über iCloud Drive synchronisieren; dann speichert Apple sie in deinem eigenen iCloud-Account. Ich habe darauf keinen Zugriff. Dafür gilt die Datenschutzrichtlinie von Apple: apple.com/de/legal/privacy.

Formeln werden direkt auf deinem Gerät gesetzt: Die dafür verwendete Bibliothek KaTeX und ihre Schriften sind in der App enthalten. Die Vorschau, der Export als PDF oder Bild und das Kopieren als Bild funktionieren deshalb auch offline, und dabei wird nichts aus dem Internet geladen oder dorthin gesendet. Links in deinen Dokumenten öffnen sich erst, wenn du sie antippst, in Safari. Die Zwischenablage nutzt die App nur, wenn du ausdrücklich „LaTeX-Code kopieren“ oder „Als Bild kopieren“ wählst.

Auch der Versionsverlauf, der private Ordner und die Einstellungen werden nur lokal auf deinem Gerät gespeichert. Einige Einstellungen (z. B. angeheftete iCloud-Dokumente, Ordnerfarben) werden über deinen iCloud-Account zwischen deinen Geräten abgeglichen. Widgets erhalten eine Liste deiner letzten Dokumente (Name und Anfang des Textes), die ebenfalls nur auf deinem Gerät liegt; bei aktiver App-Sperre wird kein Inhalt an Widgets weitergegeben.

Face ID / Touch ID: Die App-Sperre und der private Ordner nutzen die Gerätesperre von iOS. Die App erhält dabei nur die Information „erfolgreich“ oder „nicht erfolgreich“ – nie biometrische Daten.

Privacy Policy for LaTeX Editor

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the app “LaTeX Formula Editor” for iPhone and iPad (the “App”). Your privacy matters: the App doesn't need an account, and the content of your documents is never sent to me or to anyone else.

2. Your documents

Your documents are stored only on your device, in the “On My iPhone/iPad › LaTeX Editor” folder of the Files app. With LaTeX Editor Pro you can also sync them with iCloud Drive; Apple then stores them in your own iCloud account. I have no access to them. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Formulas are typeset right on your device: the KaTeX library used for this and its fonts are built into the App. The preview, exporting as PDF or image and copying as an image therefore work offline, and nothing is loaded from or sent to the internet in the process. Links in your documents only open in Safari when you tap them. The App only uses the clipboard when you explicitly choose “Copy LaTeX Code” or “Copy as Image”.

The version history, the private folder and your settings are also stored only on your device. Some settings (such as pinned iCloud documents and folder colors) are synced between your devices through your iCloud account. Widgets receive a list of your recent documents (name and beginning of the text), which also stays on your device; while the app lock is on, no content is shared with widgets.

Face ID / Touch ID: The app lock and the private folder use iOS device authentication. The App only learns whether authentication succeeded – never any biometric data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Without a subscription, the App is funded by ads, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. In particular, the following data may be processed: IP address, device and system information (such as device model, iOS version, language), approximate location (derived from the IP address), interactions with ads and – only with your permission – your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). The content of your documents is never transmitted.

Consent: Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings › Privacy Choices.

App Tracking Transparency: The advertising identifier (IDFA) is only used if you allow it in Apple's prompt. You can withdraw this permission at any time in the iOS Settings under Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Data may be transferred to countries outside the EU, in particular the United States. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

With a LaTeX Editor Pro subscription, no ads are shown and the advertising SDK isn't started.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. I don't receive payment details or any information that identifies you personally – the App only learns whether a subscription is active.

5. No other data

The App doesn't use any analytics or tracking services of its own, doesn't create user accounts and doesn't send any data to me. Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on the device; documents in iCloud Drive remain in your iCloud account.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. As I don't store any personal data myself, please also contact Google or Apple directly about data they process.

I update this privacy policy when the App or the law changes. The version published here applies.
