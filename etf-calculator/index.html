Frequently asked questions

Does the Owlvest ETF calculator consider German taxes?

Yes, with Owlvest Premium. It covers taxes for Germany, including the Vorabpauschale with the official base rate, as well as the US (taxable, Roth, Traditional) and other countries.

Can Owlvest import my portfolio from my broker?

Yes. Share your broker's CSV export with the app to import your positions, then see your real cost basis, gain so far, dividends and allocation. You can also compare your portfolio with your savings plan.

Do I need an account for the Owlvest ETF savings plan calculator?

No. No account is needed, and the calculations run on your device. With iCloud sync, your data stays in step between iPhone and iPad.

Is Owlvest free, and what does Owlvest Premium add?

A monthly or yearly subscription removes all ads and unlocks taxes, a payout plan and forecast for your real portfolio, market fluctuations from 500 simulations, positions with cost basis, cost check, target allocation and rebalancing, plus unlimited scenarios, CSV export, color themes and widgets. You can try it free for 3 days.

Is the Owlvest ETF calculator investment advice?

No. Owlvest is a simplified model based on constant returns and is not investment or tax advice. Past performance is no guarantee of future results.
