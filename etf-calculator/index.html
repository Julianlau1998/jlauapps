Owlvest: ETF Calculator

Compound Interest & Portfolio

See how your money grows – beautifully, clearly and in seconds.

Owlvest turns your ETF savings plan into a clear picture: enter your initial investment, monthly contribution, duration and expected return, and instantly see your final value, how much comes from compound interest and when you reach your next milestone.

Free · In-app purchases

Features

PLAN YOUR SAVINGS

  • Interactive growth chart – drag across it to see any year
  • Final value, money invested and returns at a glance
  • Yearly increase, fund costs (TER), inflation and today's purchasing power
  • Special payments and savings pauses, e.g. a bonus or parental leave
  • Milestones: when do you reach $100,000 or your first million?
  • Year-by-year table

REACH YOUR GOAL

  • Find out how much you need to save each month to reach a target amount
  • Compare strategies as saved scenarios side by side

PLAN YOUR PAYOUT

  • See how long your money lasts in retirement – with rising withdrawals
  • Perpetual withdrawal: how much you can take out without touching your capital

TRACK YOUR REAL PORTFOLIO

MADE FOR iOS

  • Import positions straight from your broker: share the CSV export with the app
  • Real cost basis, gain so far, dividends and allocation by position or asset class
  • Compare your portfolio with your savings plan – ahead or behind?
  • Monthly reminder and celebrations when you pass a milestone
  • Native design with Liquid Glass, dark mode and Dynamic Type
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets
  • Siri & Shortcuts: "How much will I have with Owlvest?"
  • iCloud sync across iPhone and iPad
  • Share your plan as a beautiful image
