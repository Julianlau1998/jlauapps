2. Deine Pläne, Szenarien und dein Depot

Alle Berechnungen finden lokal auf deinem Gerät statt. Deine Pläne, Einstellungen und gespeicherten Szenarien werden auf deinem Gerät gespeichert. Wenn iCloud auf deinem Gerät aktiviert ist, werden deine gespeicherten Szenarien sowie die Einträge, Positionen und Ausschüttungen in „Mein Depot“ zusätzlich in deiner privaten iCloud-Datenbank bei Apple (CloudKit) gespeichert, damit sie auf deinen Geräten gleich sind. Darauf hast nur du Zugriff; ich kann diese Daten nicht einsehen. Es gilt die Datenschutzrichtlinie von Apple.

3. Widgets, Siri, Mitteilungen, Basiszins und Depot-Import

Damit Widgets deine Daten anzeigen können, teilt die App sie über einen lokalen, geschützten Speicherbereich (App Group) auf deinem Gerät mit den Widgets. Die Integration in Siri und die Kurzbefehle-App läuft ebenfalls auf deinem Gerät. Dabei werden keine Daten an mich übertragen.

Die optionale monatliche Erinnerung, deinen Depotwert einzutragen, ist eine lokale Mitteilung auf deinem Gerät und wird nicht über einen Server verschickt.

Basiszins-Aktualisierung: Für die Berechnung der deutschen Vorabpauschale ruft die App höchstens einmal täglich die öffentliche Zinsstrukturkurve der Deutschen Bundesbank ab (api.statistiken.bundesbank.de) und berechnet daraus den Basiszins. Ist diese nicht erreichbar, lädt sie ersatzweise eine kleine Datei mit dem Basiszins von dieser Website (jlau.netlify.app). Dabei werden keine persönlichen Daten aus der App übertragen. Wie bei jedem Website-Aufruf erhalten die Deutsche Bundesbank bzw. der Hosting-Anbieter Netlify, Inc. (USA) technisch bedingt deine IP-Adresse und Verbindungsdaten, um die Daten auszuliefern (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO). Du kannst die automatische Aktualisierung in den Einstellungen der App ausschalten.

Depot-Import: Wenn du eine CSV-Datei deines Brokers importierst, wird sie ausschließlich auf deinem Gerät ausgewertet und nicht übertragen. Kurse übernimmt die App aus der Datei oder du trägst sie selbst ein; es werden keine Kursdaten von Drittanbietern abgefragt. Positionen und Ausschüttungen werden wie in Abschnitt 2 beschrieben gespeichert.

Privacy Policy for ETF Calculator

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the app “ETF Calculator” (German: “ETF-Rechner”) for iPhone and iPad (the “App”). The App lets you calculate savings plans, goals and payouts for ETFs and compare scenarios. Your numbers stay with you: all calculations run on your device, there is no server of my own and no account, and your input isn't sent to me.

Julian Lau

Germany

2. Your plans, scenarios and portfolio

All calculations happen locally on your device. Your plans, settings and saved scenarios are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled on your device, your saved scenarios and the entries, positions and distributions in “My Portfolio” are also stored in your private iCloud database at Apple (CloudKit) so they stay the same on your devices. Only you can access it; I can't see this data. Apple's Privacy Policy applies.

3. Widgets, Siri, notifications, base rate and portfolio import

So that widgets can show your data, the App shares it with them through a local, protected storage area (App Group) on your device. The Siri and Shortcuts integration also runs on your device. No data is sent to me for this.

The optional monthly reminder to record your portfolio value is a local notification on your device and isn't sent through a server.

Base rate updates: To calculate the German Vorabpauschale, the App retrieves the public yield curve of the Deutsche Bundesbank (api.statistiken.bundesbank.de) at most once a day and calculates the base rate (Basiszins) from it. If it can't be reached, it downloads a small file with the base rate from this website (jlau.netlify.app) instead. No personal data from the App is sent. As with any website request, the Deutsche Bundesbank or the hosting provider Netlify, Inc. (USA) technically receives your IP address and connection data in order to deliver the data (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can turn off automatic updates in the App's settings.

Portfolio import: If you import a CSV file from your broker, it is processed only on your device and is not transmitted. Prices are taken from the file or entered by you; the App does not request market data from third parties. Positions and distributions are stored as described in section 2.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Without Premium, the App is funded by labeled ads (native ads and occasional full-screen ads). It uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. To deliver and measure ads and – with your consent – to personalize them, this may involve processing your IP address, device identifiers, device and system information (such as device model, iOS version, language), approximate location (derived from the IP address) and usage and interaction data related to ads. Your plans, scenarios and portfolio entries are never transmitted for this.

Consent: Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent platform (User Messaging Platform, based on the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) before personalized ads are shown (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice anytime in the App under Settings › Ad Privacy Settings.

App Tracking Transparency: In addition, iOS asks once whether the App may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalized ads. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used. You can change this anytime in the iOS Settings under Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Data may be transferred to countries outside the EU, in particular the United States. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

With an ETF Calculator Premium subscription, no ads are shown.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

ETF Calculator Premium (monthly or yearly subscription) is purchased and managed entirely through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. I don't receive any payment data or information that identifies you personally – the App only learns whether there is an active subscription.

6. No other data

Apart from sections 3 and 4, the App uses no analytics or tracking services, creates no account with me and sends no data to me. Uninstalling the App deletes all data stored on your device; you can delete data in your iCloud in iOS Settings under Apple Account › iCloud.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw any consent given at any time with effect for the future. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. As I don't store any personal data myself, please also contact Google or Apple directly about data they process.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

I update this privacy policy when the App or the law changes. The version published here applies.
