Party game

HowlNight: Party Games

Werewolf, Impostor & more

Seven party games, one phone, zero setup: just pass HowlNight around the group. No extra devices, no account, no sign-up – add the players once and start playing.

iPhoneiPadFree

Features

WEREWOLF NIGHT

The app leads the night: a real narrator voice calls the roles, the werewolves pick their victim right on the device, and in the morning the village learns who didn't make it. So everyone gets to play – nobody has to be the game master. Prefer it the classic way? Then one person leads the night step by step.

  • 16 roles, from Seer and Witch to Wild Child and Bear Tamer
  • Presets for Beginner, Classic and Chaos
  • Two narrator voices, night ambience and a discussion timer
  • Continue interrupted games later

IMPOSTOR

Everyone knows the secret word – except one. Each of you says one word about it, then you vote. But watch out: a caught Impostor still wins by guessing the word.

TICKING BOMB

A category and a bomb that ticks faster and faster. Name something that fits and pass the phone on before it blows.

SAY IT DIFFERENTLY

Explain words without using the forbidden ones – in teams, against the clock. Also as charades.

WHO AM I?

Ask yes-or-no questions to find out who you are – the classic way or with the phone on your forehead.

WHO WOULD RATHER …?

One question, everyone points at someone at once. Perfect to warm up.

DRAW IT!

Draw the word, the others guess. No letters, no numbers.

MADE FOR EVERY GROUP

  • About 1,950 words, questions and categories in English and German
  • Secret cards: hold to peek, let go – and it's hidden again
  • Player photos, seating order and statistics: who's tonight's champion?
  • A result card with your own photo to share
  • Large text, haptics and sounds, for iPhone and iPad
PRO

HOWLNIGHT PRO

For free, you play all seven games with the basic packs. With Pro you get:

  • Every premium pack in every game
  • Create your own packs – with AI suggestions from Apple Intelligence right on your device
  • Extra app icons
  • No ads

As a monthly plan or a yearly plan with one week free.
