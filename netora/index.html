Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the Netora paycheck calculator?

Netora uses the official formulas of each country – for example the German Federal Ministry of Finance's wage tax algorithm, IRS and state withholding rules in the US and PAYE with National Insurance in the UK – and calculates to the cent. Results are estimates and can differ from your actual payslip, for example because of company-specific benefits; they are not tax advice.

Which countries does Netora support?

Germany, Austria, Switzerland, the United States (all 50 states and DC), the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Ireland, the Netherlands, Spain and France. New annual tax values are delivered automatically, without an app update.

Can I calculate the gross salary I need for a certain net pay?

Yes. Tap the net amount in the calculator and enter the take-home pay you want – Netora works out the gross salary you need, like a currency converter in both directions.

How does the job comparison work?

Enter a job offer and Netora compares it with your current job: net pay per month after commuting costs and net pay per hour including commuting time, plus vacation, home office days and weekly hours. Choose whether more money, more free time or a balance matters most and Netora recommends the offer that suits you. The job comparison is part of Netora Pro.

Is my salary data sent anywhere?

No. All calculations run on your device, there is no account and your salary data never leaves your iPhone or iPad.
